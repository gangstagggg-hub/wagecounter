Wage Counter

Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Wage Counter ("the app") is a simple time-and-earnings tracker. This page explains what information the app handles and how.

What the app stores

The app stores the following information only on your own device, using your browser's or device's local storage:

  • Your hourly wage
  • Start and planned end times you enter
  • Overtime rate settings you configure
  • Pause/resume state

What the app does not do

  • The app does not send any of this information to a server.
  • The app does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking of any kind.
  • The app does not collect your name, location, contacts, or any other personal data.
  • The app does not share data with third parties, because it does not collect any data to share.

Data control

Since all data stays on your device, you are always in control of it. Clearing the app's storage (through your browser or device settings, or by uninstalling the app) permanently deletes this information. There is no account, server, or backup involved.

Permissions

The app does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, location, or any other device permissions.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in the future, the updated version will be published at this same address.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed to the app developer via the contact details listed on the app's store page.
